How Restivo works

Three steps, works on any phone, no customer account needed.

01

Create your restaurant

We create /restaurant/your-slug for you. You add logo, cover, categories, items and prices. Takes ~10 minutes. Images are stored in Postgres, not ephemeral disk.

Tip: start with 8-12 items, add more after your first order.

02

Share your menu

Share the link on Instagram bio, WhatsApp status, Google listing. Print QR for tables and packaging. Use the dashboard's share + QR page.

One link works everywhere — no separate app.

03

Receive orders

Customer builds cart and submits. You get instant SSE alert in dashboard + organized order with tracking code. Update status so customer can track at /track.

Delivery fee and open hours enforced server-side.

QR→Menu→Product→Cart→Order + WhatsApp

What the customer sees

  1. Scans QR or opens link → cover, logo, open badge, categories
  2. Searches menu, taps + on price ticket — fly-to-cart animation
  3. Opens cart sheet: quantity controls, pickup/delivery toggle, address if delivery
  4. Enters name + WhatsApp (+ address for delivery), places order or opens WhatsApp with pre-filled cart
  5. Gets tracking code — tracks status on /track?code=XXXX
